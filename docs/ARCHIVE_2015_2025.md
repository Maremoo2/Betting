# Rikstoto historic market archive, 2015–2025

## Purpose and discovery

Collect genuine provider data at race/pool level. Blank periods are recorded,
not filled. No entire-day completeness requirement discards valid races.
The separate public historical discovery route is `/racedays/dates/from/to`;
ordinary results discovery can be empty while historic metadata still exists.
Both routes were found in the public website's service code.

Live census on 10 October 2026: 132 months contain 202,946 discovered race entries.
This counts metadata, not qualified races or verified outcomes. Repeated/stale
provider metadata must not be interpreted as proof of a usable betting archive.

`F1_NR_2015-06-01` exists in the historic API, but its race start is the sentinel
`0001-01-01T00:43:00` and ordinary pools are absent. Several older records have
WIN updates on later calendar dates, including 2018 races with 2019 timestamps.
Missing/sentinel starts and migrated update dates are UNKNOWN timing, even when
WIN and COL share exactly the same timestamp. Never classify these as PRIMARY.

## Commands

```powershell
python -m rikstoto_crawler.cli archive --mode census --first 2015-01 --last 2025-12 --output research-local/archive-2015-2025/full
python -m rikstoto_crawler.cli archive --mode pilot --output research-local/archive-2015-2025/pilot
python -m rikstoto_crawler.cli archive --mode full --output research-local/archive-2015-2025/full
```

Pilot and full collection require separate directories. Pilot takes up to five
races from one deterministically selected domestic meeting in the latest available
day per year, or a foreign meeting if no domestic meeting exists that day. It checks
schema behaviour, not representative yearly performance. No year is excluded from
full collection merely because its pilot lacks useful data.

Completed days resume without re-fetching; failed requests have bounded retry.
Historical collections run from the newest available day backwards. A full 11-year
job can require hundreds of thousands of requests and take days at the existing
read-only rate limit. It is a local Python process; it requires a running PC and
network. The request loop is an ordinary Python program.

## Data and exclusions

Each day is frozen in `market-days/YYYY-MM-DD.json` with its canonical checksum
**before** any outcome endpoint is opened for that day. `raw-markets/` preserves
responses and source/acquisition hashes. Completed results remain separately in
`raw-results/` and `result-days/`. Old 2026 freezes are never modified.

- FULL_WIN_AND_COLLECTIVE: every active runner has valid WIN and at least one
  complete normalized collective pool. PLACE has its own independent status.
- WIN_ONLY: complete WIN but no complete collective pool.
- RAW_OR_REJECTED: incomplete/invalid data with reasons; raw responses remain.
- VERIFIED_FULL_FIELD results require explicit isComplete=true, full active field,
  unique runner numbers, matching horse IDs/names and a verified winner.
  Top-three-only results are insufficient. Dead heats are retained and excluded
  from the simple single-winner log-loss/Brier summaries.

V76 is kept as a separate historic product and uses provider pool/leg identifiers;
missing shares never produce pCOL. Unrecognized products and multitrack/secondary
pools are recorded as unsupported. No percentage, odds or actual start is imputed.
Unknown country stays UNKNOWN; domestic NO inference is explicitly marked.

## Uploads and analysis

One MARKET ZIP per ISO week combines complete and excluded race records, with
individual-day byte hashes. ZIPs contain no outcome data. `census.json` tracks
metadata coverage; `progress.json` tracks completed days; `FINAL.json` reports
collection totals after completion. Research probabilities are pWIN/pCOL market
normalizations, never model p(win). Existing frozen seven-tier AND thresholds are
reused; PRIMARY/SECONDARY/diagnostic timing remains separate, with UNKNOWN added
for untrustworthy historic clocks. Historical signal distributions with UNKNOWN
timing are diagnostic only.

Verified WIN log-loss/Brier are descriptive retrospective research. Closing/archive
prices do not establish an executable pre-race price, and this job reports ROI as
NOT_ESTABLISHED. No stake, BET/PASS, Champion change, real-money execution, threshold
tuning or claim of prospective validation is introduced.

## Pilot completed before full expansion

The 11-year pilot collected 55 races: 45 complete WIN/COL, 8 WIN-only, 2 raw/rejected;
34 verified full-field outcomes and 29 races with both complete markets and verified
outcomes. All 11 pilot MARKET weekly ZIP manifests/checksums passed. The full
collection has 3,867 discovered dates, not a promise of 3,867 complete days.
January–May 2015 discovery was empty and remains documented as unknown coverage.

## Collection direction and resume

Full collection defaults to ascending dates (2015 to 2025). `--order descending`
retains the old operational order. Direction does not change frozen rules or data:
completed daily market/result freezes are checksum-verified and reused without
provider calls. The progress counter includes already stored days in the selected
range, even if they are later in the queue. Pilot day selection remains the latest
available day per year. Weekly packaging still occurs at full completion.

## Year folders while collection runs

A separate read-only organizer copies only committed market/result day pairs into
`archive-2015-2025-by-year/YYYY/PRE`, `YYYY/POST`, and `YYYY/weekly`.
Every year has STATUS.json; INDEX.json summarizes all years. PRE and POST weekly
ZIPs are separate. Daily copies are immutable and weekly ZIPs are atomically updated
as days arrive. Manifests contain byte checksums and actual included dates. An ISO
week spanning New Year is split into calendar-year slices, explicitly labelled.
Only discovered-date completion is reported; empty discovery is never assumed to
mean no racing, and training_ready remains false until independent QC approval.

```powershell
python -m rikstoto_crawler.organize --source research-local/archive-2015-2025/full --output research-local/archive-2015-2025-by-year --watch
```

The organizer polls every five minutes and exits after the source FINAL.json exists.
It makes no network requests and never edits the source archive. It can be stopped
or restarted independently of the crawler. Use a separate output directory: overlap
with the source is rejected. Frozen-copy discrepancies stop the organizer rather
than replacing evidence. This structure does not open or approve lockbox analysis.
