# HBI V1 Acceptance Criteria

V1 completion has two meanings that must never be mixed.

## V1 engineering completion

V1 engineering is complete when all of the following exist and pass CI:

1. canonical point-in-time database and immutable frozen predictions;
2. read-only Rikstoto field/market collection and Swedish ATG enrichment;
3. frozen Fundamental Shadow Champion and market-only baseline;
4. best-effort T-4 shadow decision path with BET/PASS/NOT_EXECUTABLE;
5. official result settlement, CLV and paper P/L;
6. Research Observatory with predictive and economic diagnostics;
7. machine-readable Research Governance and P0/P1 gates;
8. future-mutation temporal-integrity regression check;
9. frozen runtime replay parity audit;
10. settlement reconciliation audit;
11. deterministic learning dataset derived from frozen operational records;
12. run/code/config/schema provenance manifests;
13. challenger-specific prospective forward clocks;
14. recommendation-only promotion gate;
15. research-only counterfactual engine;
16. immutable nightly research backups;
17. automated V1 system-audit workflow;
18. protected strategy-file CI.

Engineering completion means the platform is capable of collecting trustworthy
prospective evidence. It does **not** mean the betting strategy is validated.

## V1 strategic validation

Strategic validation requires evidence that cannot be created by code changes alone.
It remains pending until sufficient real prospective races have accumulated.

Required evidence includes:

- P0 temporal/provider/settlement/runtime-parity gates passing on real runs;
- enough untouched prospective observations for the active Champion;
- calibration/log-loss/Brier evaluation against the market-only baseline;
- stable provider and settlement coverage;
- any future Challenger completing its own forward-validation clock.

## Current target state

After the engineering implementation is merged, the expected status is:

- V1 engineering: COMPLETE
- shadow operations: ACTIVE
- strategic validity: NOT_VALIDATED
- automatic promotion: OFF
- real-money execution: OFF

That is a successful V1 build. Evidence accumulation then happens through scheduled
shadow runs rather than continued tuning of the frozen Champion.
